About Freelance Fee Calculator & How It Works

Freelance Fee Calculator is a lightweight, secure online utility designed for developers, digital nomads, and creators working with global clients.

Key Features

  • 100% Secure & Private: All processing happens directly in your browser. Zero data transmission to external servers.
  • Multi-Platform Comparison: Side-by-side payout analysis across Upwork, PayPal, Fiverr, Stripe, and Payoneer.
  • FX Spread Loss Calculation: Accurate hidden currency margin tracking for international bank transfers.
