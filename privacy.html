Privacy Policy

At UtilinOne, we prioritize the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our web utility suite.

1. Zero Server Data Storage (100% Client-Side)

All utilities provided on UtilinOne operate entirely within your web browser (Client-Side). Any data, files, text, or images you input, process, or convert remain on your local device. We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal data or tool inputs on any external servers.

2. Cookies and Third-Party Services

We use standard third-party services to analyze site traffic and display non-intrusive advertisements:

  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymous traffic metrics to help us understand user behavior and improve site performance.
  • Google AdSense: May use cookies to serve relevant advertisements based on your interests.

3. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use our utility tools.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official support channels.
